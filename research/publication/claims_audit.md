# Claims audit checklist (M4; nothing to audit at M0)

- [ ] Every row of `research/claims_register.csv` has an evidence path that exists.
- [ ] Every numerical claim maps to EXP id, run ids, config and figure or table.
- [ ] Every external claim maps to a source whose content was read.
- [ ] Denominators, units, precision, uncertainty and sample counts cross-checked against raw outputs.
- [ ] No claim of demonstrated human learning gains.
- [ ] No unsupported causal or universal statement.
- [ ] Failed, null and exploratory results are reported and labelled.
- [ ] Second review pass that challenges the preferred hypothesis completed and recorded.
